## Questions & Answers

**Q1: How are you involved in the club?**

**Q2: What equipment does the club community share?**

**Q3: What coordination problem did you identify?**

A: P2 said: “Not every officer knows which officer lent what to the other members.” This captured the difficulty of knowing what another officer had lent out.

**Q4: What example illustrated that problem?**

A: The group described an actual occasion when a particular laptop was wanted but could not be found because another person had already borrowed or lent it. Someone looking for it might have to ask the faculty advisor, who might not have recorded or remembered who took it. The interviewer did not identify P2 as the person who personally searched for that laptop.

**Q5: How often did the group describe this happening?**

A: Similar confusion was described as occasional. Participants framed it as a minor inconvenience rather than a major incident.

**Q6: How are loans kept on record?**

A: P2 agreed with the group’s account that not everything is written down and that people sometimes rely on memory. No specific recordkeeping method used by P2 personally was recalled.

**Q7: What return expectations were discussed?**

A: The group described returning equipment once project work was finished, with a return within a week encouraged rather than a universal hard deadline. Some small components may be kept with permission; laptops and other higher-value items must return.

**Q8: What other concerns did you agree with?**

A: P2 agreed with the shared concerns about small parts going missing, equipment care, and uncertainty about current use. Small missing parts were described as inconvenient even when they were inexpensive.

**Q9: What worked reasonably well?**

A: The group described a longstanding practice of members borrowing and returning equipment. No breakage incidents were reported. The coordination difficulties coexisted with that generally functioning arrangement.

**Q10: Would you take part in later feedback?**

## Synthesis

- **One surprising quote — P2:** “Not every officer knows which officer lent what to the other members.”
- **One thing I didn’t expect:** Two officers can have different knowledge of where shared equipment is, even when they use the same lab. The treasurer’s reported concern here was coordination; no budget problem was established.
- **What this makes me think about the problem:** Equipment availability can become unclear when loan information stays with individual officers. Further research should examine how people communicate handovers and update one another.
