## Questions & Answers

**Q1: How are you involved in equipment lending?**

A: P4 lends equipment to students. Two clubs use his lab, and students know him through classes, clubs, and time spent there.

**Q2: What recent lending experience did you describe?**

A: A student’s laptop was not working that morning, and they needed a laptop to complete a project. The student visited P4’s room, explained the situation, and asked to borrow one. P4 lent one of his laptops. The specific project task was not discussed.

**Q3: What return timing was agreed?**

A: The student could keep it for the entire day. They returned it that afternoon, before the end of school.

**Q4: How was the loan recorded?**

A: P4 attached a sticky note to his desk with the student’s name and borrowed equipment. He described tracking loans through sticky notes or notepad entries.

**Q5: Was there communication during the loan?**

A: No communication occurred between handover and return. P4 already knows most borrowers through the lab, classes, or clubs. Familiarity and memory support his written notes.

**Q6: How was the laptop returned?**

A: The student came to his office, said they had finished, and returned the laptop. P4 accepted it. What happened to the loan note afterward was not asked.

**Q7: Did this loan cause concern or difficulty?**

A: P4 explicitly said he was not particularly worried because he knew the student. He did not recall another incident involving difficulty tracking equipment or getting it returned.

**Q8: How did you respond to the proposed centralized lending process?**

A: After the interviewer introduced the idea, P4 said it could keep information in one place and make lending more official through records, tracking, and timelines.

**Q9: What concern did you raise about that proposal?**

A: His main concern was encouraging people to try and use a new process. He did not identify a specific usability problem.

**Q10: Would you participate in later feedback?**

A: P4 agreed to give feedback on a storyboard or prototype.

## Synthesis

- **One notable statement — P4:** He said he was not particularly worried about lending the laptop because he knew the student.
- **One thing I didn’t expect:** A desk note supported a loan returned earlier than the latest agreed return time.
- **What this makes me think about the problem:** Familiarity and personal notes can work. Further research should examine when information needs to be accessible to others. His response to centralization followed the interviewer’s proposal and does not establish failure of his current process.
