## Summary

- **What the feedback confirmed:** Both participants reacted positively to the proposed approach. P5 understood the request, approval, collection, and return sequence and saw value in documenting it.
- **What the feedback challenged:** Approval did not explain how pickup would happen when the lender was absent. Decision and handover details needed to be clearer.
- **Changes to the storyboard:** Panel 3 adds an approval note with pickup instructions. Panel 4 shows staff checking approval, handing over the camera, and recording collection. Panel 5 adds a return-location note and a return-reported state. Panel 6 separates that report from the lender checking receipt and confirming the return.
- **Changes to the idea, if any:** Add short request and loan notes, explicit approval or rejection, and lender-arranged pickup options. The illustrated route uses staff assistance. Prepared-location pickup remains an alternative to test.
